## Wednesday 07-08 — the reskin, and the editor arc begins

- **Viewer PDF-flip fix** (`88e0c99`) — VLL field bug: pages intermittently rendered

  redraw. The behavioral confirmation was VLL's browser (unreproducible headless); the
